SummitShare, embarked on its journey and development of the current version that is currently known under the Ethereum Foundation's Next Billion project. Representing the fourth cohort, one of the core team members, Mulenga Kapwewe, played a pivotal role in its development. The culmination of these efforts was showcased at the annual Ethereum event, DevConnect, held in Istanbul during the last quarter of 2023.

DevConnect Istanbul proved to be an exciting and enriching experience. It provided a unique opportunity for Mulenga Kapwewe and other Ethereum Fellows, both from the current and previous cohorts, to meet, share their projects, exchange ideas, and gather valuable input. The event was filled with numerous sessions and activities focused on tokenization, payment platforms, ideation, and problem-solving. These sessions were instrumental in expanding knowledge and fostering discussions that could potentially shape the future of various projects, including SummitShare.

The stimulating environment at 
 allowed attendees to dive deep into the latest advancements and innovations within the Ethereum ecosystem. Mulenga found the event particularly beneficial, as it opened doors to numerous learning and networking opportunities. Engaging with other visionaries and experts in the field provided fresh perspectives and insights that were crucial for refining and enhancing the SummitShare project.

The interactions and feedback received at DevConnect played a significant role in the ongoing development of SummitShare. The exchange of ideas with other Ethereum Fellows and industry leaders not only validated the project's direction but also introduced new possibilities and improvements. The collaborative spirit and knowledge-sharing at DevConnect Istanbul underscored the importance of such events in driving innovation and progress within the blockchain community.

Overall, DevConnect Istanbul was a landmark event for SummitShare. It highlighted the project's progress and potential, reaffirmed the commitment of the team, and strengthened connections within the Ethereum community. The experiences and learnings from DevConnect continue to inspire and guide the SummitShare team as they work towards their mission of preserving and celebrating African heritage through blockchain technology.
